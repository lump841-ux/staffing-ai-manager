Temp Profiles & Invitations

Only temps who receive an agency invitation can enter the Temp Portal. Each temp completes their own profile and uploads their own photo. That real profile image follows the temp everywhere they appear: shift boards, messages, emergency replacement lists, and authorized client views.

Profile rule: invited temps stay pending until they accept the invite and complete onboarding. After they upload their own photo, that becomes their profile image throughout the system. The sample connected temps above use real-photo profile icons to demonstrate the finished experience.
