TWANOVA LLC — Workforce Communications Hub

Illustrative agency subscription and service agreement · Draft for legal review

Parties. Provider: Twanova LLC (“Twanova”). Customer: [agency name] (“Agency”).

Services. Twanova proposes hosted staffing communications, workforce status coordination, and agency/client/worker dashboard access, subject to the final statement of services, acceptable-use terms, and service limitations.

Fees. The Agency selects capacity of 1–200 active workers and Monthly Flex at $349 monthly, plus a $1,499 one-time connection and onboarding fee due at signup. The first monthly subscription charge begins one calendar month after activation. Taxes and optional services, if applicable, will be disclosed in the final order form.

Term. Monthly subscription; cancellation and renewal rules to be set out in the final agreement. Contract start date, effective date, first billing date, payment due dates, automatic renewal treatment, and early termination rights require final approval.

Agency responsibilities. Agency will be responsible for authorized users, accurate worker rosters, appropriate permissions, and lawful handling of worker and client information.

Data and security. Privacy, data ownership, access controls, processing arrangements, retention and export rules, breach notification, and data deletion obligations must be specified before execution.

Commercial and legal protections. Service levels, permitted uses, availability, support, warranty disclaimers, limits of liability, indemnities, governing law, notices, dispute process, and changes to these terms must be reviewed by legal counsel.

Electronic signatures. The final execution version will require verification of the authorized signer's identity, consent to electronic records, a tamper-evident signed document, and downloadable copies for both parties.

Important: This draft does not replace an attorney-reviewed agreement and cannot be accepted as a legally operative contract.
